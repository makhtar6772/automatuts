Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

At AutomaTuts.site ("we", "us", "our"), available at https://automatuts.site, your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: if you contact us, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Information collected automatically: like most websites, we and our service providers may record your IP address, browser type, device, operating system, referring page, pages visited and visit time.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website
  • To respond to your messages
  • To understand how visitors use the site
  • To display advertising
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security problems

3. Cookies and web beacons

We use cookies to remember preferences and to understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly if you do.

4. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Third-party privacy policies

This policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Please read their privacy policies for details about their practices and how to opt out. We cannot control the cookies used by third-party advertisers.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics services such as Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, for example pages viewed and time spent on the site.

7. European visitors (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing and to data portability. Where consent is required for personalized advertising, you may withdraw it at any time. To exercise these rights, email contact@automatuts.site.

8. California residents (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion and to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email contact@automatuts.site.

9. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

11. External links

Our articles may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@automatuts.site or use our contact page.
