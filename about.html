About AutomaTuts

Practical AI automation, explained in plain language.

AutomaTuts.site is a tech blog about using artificial intelligence and automation to get more done with less effort. We write step-by-step tutorials, tool reviews and workflow guides that you can follow even if you have never written a line of code.

Our mission

AI is changing how people work, but most guides are either too technical or too vague. Our mission is to make automation understandable and useful for students, freelancers, small business owners and professionals.

What you will find here

  • Step-by-step tutorials for building automations with no-code tools
  • Honest tool guides that explain what a tool does well and where it falls short
  • Prompt and workflow tips you can apply the same day
  • Productivity systems for writing, email, research and content creation

Our principles

  • Clarity first. Short sentences, real examples, no jargon without explanation.
  • Accuracy. We test what we recommend and update posts when tools change.
  • Transparency. If a page contains ads or affiliate links, we say so.
  • Respect for your time. Every guide aims to save you more time than it takes to read.
