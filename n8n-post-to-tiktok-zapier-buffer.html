How to Post to TikTok from n8n for Free with Zapier and Buffer

Published October 29, 2026 · 7 min read

If you use n8n, you have probably searched for a TikTok node and found nothing. There is no easy built-in way to post videos to TikTok from n8n.

This guide shows a free workaround. n8n adds a row to Google Sheets, Zapier notices the new row, and Buffer posts the video to your TikTok account.

Why posting to TikTok from n8n is hard

TikTok does have an official content posting API, but it is not meant for people who want to automate their own content. To use it, you need a verified website and an app verified by TikTok.

It is built for real apps, such as video editors that let you post directly to TikTok. That leaves third-party tools.

Paid alternatives

Several services can post to TikTok for you, but most charge a starter fee. These were the prices in the video, so check the current ones before you decide:

  • Blotato: starts at about $29 per month and allows up to 900 TikTok posts per month
  • Upload-Post: has a free plan, but TikTok needs the basic plan at around $19 per month
  • Another tool: has a free plan without TikTok, and TikTok costs $149 per month

Why Buffer, even without an API

Buffer has a free plan that supports TikTok posting with unlimited videos. The catch is that Buffer has no public API right now. The only way to reach it from automation is through Zapier.

How the workaround works

  1. Your n8n workflow adds a row to a Google Sheet with the video URL and the title.
  2. A Zapier workflow (called a Zap) triggers when a new row appears.
  3. Zapier sends the video to Buffer.
  4. Buffer posts it to TikTok.

The Google Sheet is just a bridge between n8n and Zapier.

Is it really free?

Yes, if you stay on the free plans. Zapier's free plan gives you 100 tasks per month. Each post uses one task, so that is about 100 TikToks a month, or roughly three per day.

One thing to know: after a row is added, the video can take up to 15 minutes to show up on TikTok.

Step 1: Create the Google Sheet

Make a new Google Sheet to move data between n8n and Zapier. It needs two columns:

  • Column A: video URL
  • Column B: title

In the video, the file is named "n8n to Zapier to Buffer to TikTok" and uses the default tab, Sheet1.

Step 2: Add the Google Sheets node in n8n

Open the n8n workflow where you want to post to TikTok. Add a Google Sheets node and choose the Append Row in Sheet action.

  1. Select your document (the sheet you just made).
  2. Select the sheet, which is Sheet1.
  3. Under values to send, map your video URL to the video column and your title to the title column.

That is all n8n needs to do. This one node is your "post to TikTok" node.

Step 3: Connect TikTok to Buffer

  1. Create a free Buffer account.
  2. In the channels list on the left, choose TikTok and click Connect TikTok.
  3. A page opens showing your TikTok account. Make sure you are already logged in to TikTok in another tab.
  4. Click Finish connection.

Your TikTok account now appears in the left panel, and Buffer is ready to post.

Step 4: Build the Zap in Zapier

Create a free Zapier account. In the top left, click Create and then Zaps.

Set the trigger

  1. Choose Google Sheets as the trigger app.
  2. For the trigger event, select New Spreadsheet Row.
  3. Pick your Google account. If it is not connected yet, log in as you would for any other app.
  4. Select your spreadsheet and your worksheet (Sheet1), then continue.

Before you move on, make sure the sheet is clean and do not change its columns afterward. Editing the sheet while the Zap is on can cause bugs, so let n8n add all the rows.

When Zapier asks you to test the trigger, it will say that no rows were found. That is fine. Skip the test and continue.

Set the Buffer action

  1. Search for Buffer and choose the action event Add to Queue.
  2. Log in to your Buffer account and continue.
  3. For Organization, select your organization.
  4. For Channel, select your TikTok account.
  5. For Text, map column B (the title).
  6. For Media, choose Video, and in the video box map column A (the video URL).
  7. For Method, choose Share Now so the video is published right away.

Continue, test the step and publish the Zap. Zapier may run real actions during a test, so use a video you are happy to post.

Step 5: Test the whole thing

Run your n8n workflow. The new row appears in your Google Sheet. Within about 15 minutes, the video shows up on your TikTok account with its title and audio.

In the demo, the creator used a Set node with a video URL and a title just for testing. In your own workflow, you would connect the Append Row node to wherever your video and title come from.

Quick summary

  • n8n has no TikTok node, and TikTok's official API requires a verified app.
  • A free workaround is n8n to Google Sheets to Zapier to Buffer to TikTok.
  • The free plans allow about 100 posts a month, or three a day.
  • Posts can take up to 15 minutes to appear.
  • Keep the Google Sheet clean and untouched once the Zap is on.
